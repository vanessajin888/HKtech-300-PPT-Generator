# Our Story

PPT Cursor began in March 2026 with a simple frustration: a friend of mine frequently spent hours manually editing and updating PowerPoint presentations. She wondered whether AI could automate these repetitive tasks while preserving the original formatting and structure.

That idea sparked our collaboration.

Together, we explored the concept of an AI-powered PowerPoint editor that could understand natural-language instructions and modify existing presentations. My friend contributed the original idea and product feedback, while I was responsible for developing and implementing the working prototype.

We hoped to turn this idea into something genuinely useful, and submitted the project to the HKTech300 entrepreneurship programme.

However, as we were developing our prototype, Microsoft was already advancing similar capabilities through Copilot for PowerPoint. By the time we revisited the project, Copilot had evolved into a far more mature solution than our experimental prototype.

It was a little disappointing to discover that the problem we had been working on was already being addressed by a major technology company. Nevertheless, we were glad to see that the need we identified was real.

Rather than leaving our prototype forgotten on a local drive, we decided to make the original version publicly available for anyone interested in exploring, learning from, or building upon it.

This repository preserves our original work from March 2026. It is not intended to compete with Microsoft Copilot, but to document a small experiment that began with a real-world problem, a shared idea, and our curiosity about what AI could make possible.
